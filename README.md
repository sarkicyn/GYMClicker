📝 SHORT DESCRIPTION

This project is a Unity-based game with training mechanics where player progression depends on strength gained through repetitions. 

The core gameplay focuses on building strength, overcoming thresholds, and leveling up based on performance.



🕒 GAME TIMELINE

A beginner enters his first gym with the goal of improving himself. He starts training and gradually goes through different stages of development, becoming stronger both physically and mentally.

Over time, he meets a coach who helps him unlock his potential and guides him through his training. The journey becomes more serious and requires consistent self-discipline and effort.

The protagonist takes part in various competitions, facing more experienced opponents and steadily gaining experience. Each challenge brings him closer to his main goal.

In the final stage, he gets the opportunity to compete in the Mr. Olympia tournament — the peak of a bodybuilder’s career.

The game reflects the idea of self-improvement through hard work and discipline — a path often referred to as the “American Dream.”

The player experiences this journey alongside the character, going through growth, challenges, and steady progress.



  🎮 GAMEPLAY MECHANIKS

The player controls a character whose progression is based on strength, endurance, and gym status.

💪 Level & Strength System

The character’s level depends on total strength. Strength is gained through completed exercise repetitions.
Each repetition increases strength.
The heavier the weight on the bar, the more strength is gained per rep.
However, heavier weights also cause faster fatigue.
Fatigue is represented by a stamina slider, which decreases during training and recovers over time.

⚡ Power Points System

The player earns Power Points through progression. These points can be spent on upgrades that improve training efficiency and overall character development.

🏋️ Gym Progression

Every 20 levels, the character moves to a more elite gym.
New gyms unlock new exercises and equipment.
Training becomes more difficult but more effective.

🧍 Character Appearance

The character’s appearance changes as progression increases.
The body becomes more muscular and developed.
Visual changes reflect strength growth and training progress.

🏆 Gym Status System

The character has a gym status level, which depends on the player’s choices in random side events.
A higher status increases Power Points gain.
Player decisions directly affect reputation and progression efficiency.

🥇 Competitions

Throughout the game, the character participates in competitions of varying importance.
Performance depends on preparation and training before the competition.
Preparation is determined by whether the player has reached the required strength threshold before the event.
If the character exceeds the required strength level, they perform better.
If the strength threshold is not met, performance is weaker even with significant training.
Higher placements provide better rewards and bonuses.
